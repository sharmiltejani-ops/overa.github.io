In short: OVERA Launcher has no internet connection, no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no servers. It does not send your data off your device.

1. Overview

OVERA (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides the OVERA Launcher Android application (the “App”). This policy explains what the App does with information on your device. It applies to the App only; the OVERA website has its own separate policy.

By using the App, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

2. Information we handle

We do not collect, transmit, or store personal information on any server. The App has no INTERNET permission and contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or data-collection SDKs.

The App reads and stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Installed apps list: The App reads basic information about apps that have a launcher icon (app label, package name, and icon) from Android's PackageManager so it can show your app drawer and alphabet index. This list is never uploaded or shared.
  • Recently opened apps: When you tap an app in the drawer, its package name is saved locally (up to 30 entries) in private app storage to power the “Recents” screen.
  • Preferences you choose: Your selected wallpaper, clock style, notch and alphabet display settings, and saved widget ID are stored locally in private app storage (SharedPreferences, MODE_PRIVATE).

The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, account details, payment information, precise location, photos, contacts, messages, or call history.

3. Device permissions

  • SET_WALLPAPER: Used only to apply a wallpaper that you explicitly select in the App. The App does not change your wallpaper without your action.
  • Default launcher role: The App shows an optional “Set as Default” screen that opens the Android system settings. Choosing OVERA as your home launcher is entirely your decision and can be changed at any time in system settings.
  • App visibility (package queries): The App asks Android for visibility of launchable apps and app widgets strictly to build the app drawer and widget picker. It does not request broad QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES access.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs, location, storage, notifications, accessibility services, or device identifiers such as IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID, or MAC address.

4. Cookies and analytics

The App does not use cookies, tracking storage, analytics, advertising identifiers, or third-party SDKs. It contains no embedded browser or web content that sets cookies.

Your use of external apps opened from the App, such as your browser or email app, is governed by those apps' own privacy policies.

5. How information is used

Locally stored information is used only to provide the App's core launcher features: displaying your apps, launching apps you tap, showing recently opened apps, applying your chosen wallpaper and clock style, restoring your widget, and drawing the alphabet/notch layout.

No information is used for advertising, profiling, marketing, or automated decision-making.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties. The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or social SDKs.

Some App features open other apps through standard Android intents. These actions leave the App and are governed by the destination's own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Store, when you rate the App or share its store link;
  • Your device's email app, when you choose to send feedback;
  • Your browser, when you open a link from the sample News screen (which links to Google News), this Privacy Policy, or another external page.

These links are opened only after you tap them. The App does not send them any data about you.

7. Data retention and security

All locally stored preferences and recent-app entries remain on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Android app backup and cloud/device-transfer extraction are disabled for the App (allowBackup="false"), so this data is not backed up to Google Drive or transferred as part of app data backup.

Because the App has no server and no internet access, there is no online account or database to breach. You can remove all locally stored data at any time through Android Settings → Apps → OVERA → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region), and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Since the App collects no personal information at all, there is no child data for us to retain or share.

If you believe a child has provided information through a service linked from the App, please contact us.

9. Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect, use, store on a server, or share personal information, requests for access, correction, deletion, or portability of personal data do not apply to the App.

You remain in full control of the local data: you can clear recent apps and preferences through the App's settings where available, or clear all app data / uninstall as described above. You can also choose not to set OVERA as your default launcher and continue using your previous home app.

10. Data safety & policy changes

For the purposes of Google Play's Data Safety declaration: the App does not collect any data and does not share any data. It has no internet permission, no third-party SDKs, and no server-side processing.

We may update this policy if the App's features or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when we do. The current version applies to OVERA Launcher version 1.0.
