Important: This is a general website privacy-policy template for the current static OVERA HTML website. Review it against your actual hosting, analytics, forms, app features, and applicable laws before publishing. Remove or update any statement that does not match your setup.

1. Overview

OVERA (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how information may be handled when you visit the OVERA website (“Website”). The current website is intended to present information and visual previews of the OVERA experience.

By using the Website, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please stop using the Website.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide: The current static Website does not include an account-registration system, contact form, payment feature, or user-upload feature. It therefore does not ask you to submit personal information through the page itself.

Basic technical information: The hosting provider may automatically process technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs to deliver and protect the Website. Whether this occurs, and how long such logs are kept, depends on your hosting provider and configuration.

Clock information: The live clock uses the date and time available on your own device and displays it in your browser. The website code does not send that clock value to an OVERA server.

3. Device permissions

The current static Website does not request device permissions. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, location, Bluetooth, or notifications.

The live clock uses the browser's standard local date/time function; it does not request location permission to determine your time zone. Your browser and operating system may have their own controls and privacy settings.

If future versions add features that require permissions or access to personal data, this policy should be updated before those features are released.

4. Cookies and analytics

The supplied HTML website does not intentionally set cookies, use local storage, or include third-party analytics or advertising scripts. Your hosting provider, content-delivery network, or security service may use technical logs or similar technologies depending on the services you enable.

If you later add analytics, advertising, embedded media, login, or other third-party tools, those services may use cookies or similar technologies. Update this policy to identify those services and explain available choices.

5. How information is used

Any technical information processed by the hosting or security provider may be used to:

  • deliver the Website and its resources;
  • maintain reliability and diagnose errors;
  • detect abuse, malicious traffic, or security incidents; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations.

The current page does not use submitted personal information for marketing because it does not provide a form or account feature.

6. Sharing and third-party services

OVERA does not sell personal information collected through the current static Website. Website files may be delivered by a hosting provider or content-delivery network, which may process technical data as part of providing the service.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the Website, its users, or legal rights. If you add third-party services, their own privacy policies and terms may apply.

7. Data retention and security

The current static page does not maintain a user profile or a database of visitor accounts. Technical logs, if generated by your hosting provider, are retained according to that provider's settings and policies.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect the Website and its infrastructure. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

The Website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the current page does not provide a registration or submission form, it does not intentionally ask children to provide personal details. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service connected to the Website, contact the site operator so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. These rights may be subject to legal limits.

For technical logs held by your hosting provider, requests may need to be directed to that provider. You can also manage cookies and site data through your browser settings, if applicable.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Website, its services, or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when an update is made. Please review this page periodically.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the OVERA website operator using the official contact details published on the Website or in the app listing.

Before publishing: Replace this contact instruction with a monitored privacy email address or business contact method. Do not publish the policy with missing or inaccurate contact details if your applicable law or platform requires them.
